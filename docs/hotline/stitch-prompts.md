# HOTLINE Stitch prompts

Four paste ready prompts for Google Stitch. Colors, type and do-nots come from `DESIGN.md`.

## How to use

- Start a new Stitch project.
- Pick the mobile screen type for Prompts 1 and 4.
- Paste one block per screen.
- If Stitch accepts an image attach, use `.planning/phases/02-hotline-base-playable/witch-pots-layout-ref.jpg` and say it is for layout only.
- Refine with the follow ups under each prompt.
- Swap the stand in font for Retro Computer when building.

## Prompt 1: full game screen

```text
Mobile portrait slot game screen called HOTLINE, a 5x4 reel slot with 40 lines, Hotline Miami inspired, original art.

Layout top to bottom:
1. Top bar: balance with a $ icon and a small Last win value on the left, Menu and Sound buttons on the right.
2. Big HOTLINE title in neon pink #FF2E93 with a dark outline #1A0B2E.
3. Three vintage rotary phones in one row, green left, red center, purple right. Each has a cyan #39FFE8 meter bar and a small label: 24 FREE SPINS, HOLD AND WIN, LOCKERS.
4. A dirty CRT/VHS monitor bezel holding a 5 columns by 4 rows reel grid on dark glass #0A0614. Symbols: an original animal mask Wild, pistol, cassette tape, 80s sports car, neon letters A K Q J and 10.
5. Bottom HUD on night #12061F with panels #1A0A2E and ice #9BE7FF borders: small round info button I, buttons TURBO, AUTO and 2x on the right, then a bet row with minus, bet value with a $ sign, plus, and a big round pink SPIN button #FF4DA6.

Background: motel rooftop at sunset behind the monitor, violet to pink to orange sky.
Colors: pink #FF2E93 and cyan #39FFE8 are the hero neons, gold #FFE566 only for money.
Type: uppercase pixel font, use VT323 or Press Start 2P, uppercase labels and numbers.
Mood: 1989 Miami, synthwave sunset, neon tube signage, CRT phosphor glow, VHS tracking lines, scanlines, grimy, high contrast, flat graphic shapes.
Avoid: green fantasy art, witches, cauldrons, pumpkins, cartoon casino cliches like raining coins, cherries, sevens, bells or lever, Inter or Roboto or system fonts, recognizable Hotline Miami characters or logos, glossy 3D, stock photo look.
```

Follow ups:

- Desktop variant: same screen as a centered tall column, with the rooftop and sunset filling the sides.
- Win state: a pink #FF2E93 line across three cells, those cells framed, a gold #FFE566 amount callout over the grid.
- Feature state: the green phone glowing, a FREE SPINS counter in the HUD.
