# HOTLINE Stitch prompts

Four paste ready prompts for Google Stitch. Colors, type and do-nots come from `DESIGN.md`.

## How to use

- Start a new Stitch project.
- Pick the mobile screen type for Prompts 1 and 4.
- Paste one block per screen.
- If Stitch accepts an image attach, use `.planning/phases/02-hotline-base-playable/witch-pots-layout-ref.jpg` and say it is for layout only.
- Refine with the follow ups under each prompt.
- Swap the stand in font for Retro Computer when building.

## Prompt 1: full game screen

```text
Mobile portrait slot game screen called HOTLINE, a 5x4 reel slot with 40 lines, Hotline Miami inspired, original art.

Layout top to bottom:
1. Top bar: balance with a $ icon and a small Last win value on the left, Menu and Sound buttons on the right.
2. Big HOTLINE title in neon pink #FF2E93 with a dark outline #1A0B2E.
3. Three vintage rotary phones in one row, green left, red center, purple right. Each has a cyan #39FFE8 meter bar and a small label: 24 FREE SPINS, HOLD AND WIN, LOCKERS.
4. A dirty CRT/VHS monitor bezel holding a 5 columns by 4 rows reel grid on dark glass #0A0614. Symbols: an original animal mask Wild, pistol, cassette tape, 80s sports car, neon letters A K Q J and 10.
5. Bottom HUD on night #12061F with panels #1A0A2E and ice #9BE7FF borders: small round info button I, buttons TURBO, AUTO and 2x on the right, then a bet row with minus, bet value with a $ sign, plus, and a big round pink SPIN button #FF4DA6.

Background: motel rooftop at sunset behind the monitor, violet to pink to orange sky.
Colors: pink #FF2E93 and cyan #39FFE8 are the hero neons, gold #FFE566 only for money.
Type: uppercase pixel font, use VT323 or Press Start 2P, uppercase labels and numbers.
Mood: 1989 Miami, synthwave sunset, neon tube signage, CRT phosphor glow, VHS tracking lines, scanlines, grimy, high contrast, flat graphic shapes.
Avoid: green fantasy art, witches, cauldrons, pumpkins, cartoon casino cliches like raining coins, cherries, sevens, bells or lever, Inter or Roboto or system fonts, recognizable Hotline Miami characters or logos, glossy 3D, stock photo look.
```

Follow ups:

- Desktop variant: same screen as a centered tall column, with the rooftop and sunset filling the sides.
- Win state: a pink #FF2E93 line across three cells, those cells framed, a gold #FFE566 amount callout over the grid.
- Feature state: the green phone glowing, a FREE SPINS counter in the HUD.

## Prompt 2: symbol sheet

```text
Square art board for a slot game called HOTLINE, a 4 columns by 3 rows grid of equal cells on flat night #12061F so each item is easy to cut out. Same padding and visual weight in every cell, thick silhouettes that read at 128 px, two or three tone shading with a neon rim light.

Row one: Wild, an original animal mask with cracked plastic and a pink #FF2E93 rim, the strongest read on the sheet; a pistol; a cassette tape; an 80s sports car in front three quarter view.
Row two: neon letters A, K, Q, J in pink #FF2E93 and cyan #39FFE8.
Row three: neon number 10, then three 3.5 inch diskettes: green #7DFFB3, red #FF3B3B and purple #C44DFF.

Type for letters: chunky pixel era uppercase.
Mood: 1989 Miami, neon tube signage, CRT phosphor glow, grimy, high contrast, flat graphic shapes, hot pink and cyan.
Avoid: green fantasy art, witches, cauldrons, pumpkins, cartoon casino cliches like cherries, sevens, bells or clovers, recognizable Hotline Miami characters, masks or logos, Endorphina or Witch Pots branding, glossy 3D, stock photo look, scenery or backgrounds inside cells.
```

Follow ups:

- Push the mask contrast so it reads first on the board.
- Show one symbol alone on the flat background for export.
- Check the whole set at small size, 128 px per cell.

## Prompt 4: VHS gamble overlay (optional)

Mobile portrait screen for a slot game called HOTLINE, a gamble overlay. The game is dimmed behind a deep #0B0420 layer with VHS pause tracking noise and a PAUSE style on screen display.

Centered panel styled like a VHS tape label or VCR menu, in a 640x720 proportion, panel color #1A0A2E with a pink #FF4DA6 border. Rows: title VHS GAMBLE, then CURRENT WIN and GAMBLE TO WIN with values in gold #FFE566, then an empty result line. Below, two buttons in a 320x128 proportion: SURVIVE in green #7DFFB3 and DIE in red #FF3B3B. Button and row text is page text, not baked into art.

Type: uppercase pixel font, use VT323 or Press Start 2P.
Mood: 1989 Miami, VHS tracking lines, scanlines, chromatic aberration, CRT phosphor glow, grimy, high contrast, flat graphic shapes.
Avoid: playing cards, card compare, dice, roulette, green fantasy art, cartoon casino cliches like raining coins, cherries or sevens, Inter or Roboto or system fonts, recognizable Hotline Miami characters or logos, Endorphina or Witch Pots branding, glossy 3D, stock photo look.
